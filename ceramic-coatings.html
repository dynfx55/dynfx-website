What it is

Permanent gloss.
effortless care.

A ceramic coating is a clear, hardened shell that bonds chemically to your clear coat, replacing the need for wax forever. Once cured, it produces a depth of gloss that wax can't approach, repels water and contaminants on contact, and resists chemical etching from things like bird droppings, brake dust, and tree sap.

Washing becomes effortless. Maintenance becomes minimal. The car looks better, longer.

Every coating service we perform begins with thorough decontamination and paint correction tailored to your vehicle's condition. We don't apply a coating onto a flawed surface — that locks in the imperfections. We correct first, then coat. The result is paint that looks better on day one than it has since the car left the factory.

Ceramic Coatings,
made for Michigan.

Our Livonia studio serves clients throughout metro Detroit — Plymouth, Northville, Westland, Canton, Farmington Hills, Novi, Dearborn, and the surrounding region. Every coating includes the paint correction your finish deserves.

Michigan is hard on paint. Salt-laden winter roads, freeze-thaw cycles, summer sun, and tree sap from spring through fall — all of it accelerates the breakdown of factory clear coat. A ceramic coating creates a sacrificial layer that absorbs that damage instead of your paint.

Most coatings are completed within 3–5 days, including correction. We'll walk you through the process, recommend the right tier, and have your vehicle back to you better than it arrived.
