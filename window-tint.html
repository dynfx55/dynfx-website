What it is

More than
a shade.

Window tint is more than aesthetics. The right ceramic film cuts cabin temperature meaningfully, blocks up to 99% of UV radiation, reduces glare, and protects your interior from the sun damage that turns dashboards brittle and seats faded over time.

It also looks fantastic. The right shade transforms how a vehicle reads on the road — sharper, more confident, more intentional.

We exclusively install Autobahn ceramic films — multi-layer nano-ceramic technology that doesn't interfere with cell signals, doesn't bubble or fade, and carries a lifetime transferable warranty. We carry three tiers so you only pay for the level of performance you actually want.

where you live.

Our Livonia studio serves clients throughout metro Detroit — Plymouth, Northville, Westland, Canton, Farmington Hills, Novi, Dearborn, and beyond. Most installs are completed same-day in our climate-controlled studio.

Michigan summers and Michigan sun aren't gentle on interiors. Black leather hits 140°F+ on a parked car in July. Ceramic tint cuts that meaningfully — and protects your dash, seats, and skin from the UV that does the long-term damage.

Michigan tint laws allow non-reflective film of any darkness on rear and side windows behind the driver. Front side windows must allow more than 35% light through, and the windshield can have non-reflective tint above the AS-1 line. We'll guide you through legal compliance for your specific situation.
