What it is

Invisible armor for
your finish.

Paint protection film — PPF — is a clear, self-healing urethane layer that bonds to your paint and absorbs damage that would otherwise reach your factory finish. Rock chips on the highway. Door dings in parking lots. Bug etching, road salt, brake dust, washing-induced micro-scratches.

The film takes the hit. Your paint stays untouched.

We install Autobahn Incognito PPF, a top-tier optically clear film with a hydrophobic top coat and a lifetime transferable warranty. When applied properly — by hand, in a climate-controlled studio — it's invisible to anyone who doesn't know to look for it.
